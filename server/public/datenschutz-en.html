3. What we do not do

  • No advertising or analytics trackers. The app does not include Google Analytics, advertising networks or social media pixels.
  • No sharing for advertising purposes and no sale of data.
  • No tracking of where you come from. If a link carries a channel (e.g. ?k=tiktok), we only count the total of visits and new accounts per channel and day — your account does not store the channel, and no IP address is stored for this.
  • No patient data. The platform is a professional network between businesses. It is not intended for recording details about individual patients and never asks for such details.
  • No payment card data with us. Card and wallet data are entered and processed exclusively by licensed payment service providers.

4. Where the data is stored

The application runs on servers in Frankfurt am Main (EU); so does the database. In normal operation there is no transfer to third countries.

The exceptions are the services listed in section 5, if you use them.

5. Integrated services

These services only become active if you actually use them:

  • Sign-in with Google (optional): Google learns that you are signing in to ApoPulse. We receive your email address and your name from Google.
  • Sign-in with Facebook (optional): as with Google — Meta learns that you are signing in to ApoPulse; we receive your name and, if confirmed by Facebook, your email address.

Official shortage lists (e.g. from medicines authorities) are fetched by the server, not by your device. The authorities therefore learn nothing about individual users.

6. Storage period

Account data is stored for as long as the account exists. After deletion it is removed; posts you have published may remain in anonymised form where others have referred to them.

IP addresses from the sign-in lock are kept only for the duration of the respective lock and are not stored permanently.

Technical error reports of the app are deleted 30 days after they last occurred. To limit mass reports, the IP address is counted in working memory for at most one hour and is not stored.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR).

  • Data export: in the app under “Profile → Export data”.
  • Delete account: in the app under “Profile → Delete account”.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — in Austria with the Datenschutzbehörde, in Germany with the authority of your federal state. If you live outside the EU (e.g. in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya or Senegal), you can also contact the data protection authority of your country.

8. Note on the professional content

Shortage and medicine reports come from official sources or from reports by other businesses. Every statement visibly shows its origin: verified, reference data or community report. ApoPulse gives no therapy, dosage or substitution advice and does not replace a professional assessment of the individual case.
